ROUTE DEFINITIONS

building_specific
Use for questions about the user's own recorded facts, documents, measured values, building-specific calculations, or explicitly tailored prioritization. Use when the user voluntarily supplies a concrete BRF name, street address, or building ID for the current advisory request, so identity can be resolved. If the user answers a building-selection or identity question, continue that request. If a building is already selected and the user continues its advice, keep that context.
Identity is not required to recognize this route: a request for the building's own facts without identity belongs here so clarification can obtain identity. A selected building does not make an explicitly general explanation building-specific.

Identity precision:
- A building ID is the identity; street addresses are lookup aliases, not proof of separate buildings.
- a concrete BRF name must look like an actual proper name after BRF. Prepositions, descriptive phrases, and question fragments do not form names. For example, "BRF with high heating bills" and "BRF do we have" are not proper names.
- The word “BRF” alone is not a building identifier.
- In "Hur kan vår BRF minska uppvärmningskostnaderna?", BRF describes the association type and "minska uppvärmningskostnaderna" is the requested action, never its name. Route this as generic when no concrete identity or request for recorded building facts is present. Apply the same rule to "vår BRF spara energi" and "vår BRF prioritera åtgärder".
- Board roles, budget constraints, proposals and forgotten-name statements do not supply an association name. "BRF board prioritize energy measures", "BRF replace the windows", "BRF reduce its energy bills", and "I don't remember the BRF name" are not names. In Swedish, "vår BRF alltid tjänar på", "BRF undersöka", "BRF söka" and "vår BRF blir väldigt varma" are sentence fragments, not names. Preserve genuine multiword names, numbers, Swedish characters and legal location suffixes.
- Apply the same name check in Swedish, including informal spelling without å/ä/ö. "BRF har fjärrvärme och det blir dyrt", "brf har fjarrvarme o det blir dyrt", and "vår BRF med höga värmekostnader" describe a situation, not a named association. Keep broadly applicable advice generic. "BRF Sjöstaden 1 har fjärrvärme" supplies the name "Sjöstaden 1"; do not include the following observation in the name.
- Do not infer an address or building identity from an organization type, municipality, observation, or historical assistant guess.
- During a pending address lookup, a city/municipality reply or a listed building ID continues building_specific. Resolve ambiguity in two stages: obtain location when distinct buildings span municipalities, then request a listed building ID if several buildings remain in that location. Keep the original question active; neither reply is a new general question or a portfolio request.

generic
Use for general energy explanations and broadly applicable advice when the user has not requested their building's own records, calculations, or tailored prioritization. Words such as we, our building, our roof, or our heating bills do not by themselves demand identity or a database query. The advisor can discuss goals, systems, observations, constraints, and sensible checks without an identified building.
Public/statistical/entity questions about BRFs and housing organizations remain generic unless the user explicitly requests analysis of the system's building dataset or portfolio. This includes questions such as "How many BRFs are in Stockholm?" and "How many buildings does StockholmsHem have?" Classification does not imply that a factual answer is available.
Swedish examples include "Vad är en BRF?", "Vad betyder BRF?", "Hur många BRFer finns i Stockholm?", and "Hur kan vi minska våra värmekostnader?". A request for the user's recorded energy class, such as "Vilken energiklass har vår byggnad?", remains building_specific.

conversational
Use for greetings, thanks, small talk, and questions about the assistant itself when there is no substantive energy request or continuation of one. A short observation or answer to an energy-advice follow-up is not small talk.

cluster
Use for requested comparisons, aggregations, or analysis across multiple buildings in the system's dataset, a defined group, or a user portfolio. A BRF with unresolved candidate buildings is an identity-resolution case for building_specific, not automatically a portfolio-analysis request. Do not select cluster merely because general advice mentions buildings in the plural.

Use when the user asks SPARA to create, prepare, compile, export, or download a new energy report or board briefing from the discussion. Recognizing this intent does not establish that the report has enough evidence or that a file exists; the workflow must check readiness and completion.
Swedish compounds and inflections such as "rapportutkast", "rapportutkastet", "energirapportutkast" and "styrelseunderlaget" name this same output. A plain conversational summary is not a report request; negation and requests to read an existing draft remain respected.
Requests to find, read, explain, or quote an existing declaration, audit, inspection, or other source document are not requests to create a new report. Route those by whether they concern a particular building or general guidance.

expert_handoff
Use when the user requests contact, forwarding, referral, or transfer of the discussion to a human advisor or EKR. The intent can be expressed naturally without a prescribed phrase, but must actually seek human involvement. Advice requests, difficult questions, frustration, mentioning an advisor, or asking what an advisor does do not by themselves request handoff.
This route requests a preview/confirmation workflow. It does not authorize sending and never establishes delivery. Cancellation or changed conditions must be respected by that workflow, not interpreted as consent.
